About

Analytics with both technical depth and business context.

Am I open to international roles?

Yes. For larger BI ownership, reporting transformation, or long-term analytics roles, full-time makes sense. For focused dashboard development, reporting cleanup, automation, or consulting support, part-time or project-based collaboration can also work well.
