About

Analytics with both technical depth and business context.

My strongest area is connecting data structure with real business meaning — what the metric actually means, how it should be calculated, and how stakeholders should act on it.

I am a Belgrade-based Power BI / Data Analyst with experience across financial reporting, SaaS analytics, project controlling, and business intelligence.

My work covers the full reporting lifecycle: SQL extraction, Power Query transformation, data modelling, DAX measures, dashboard UX, KPI interpretation, and automation of recurring reporting processes.

Fast answers before the first call.

These answers are here to make screening easier for recruiters, hiring managers, and clients who want to quickly understand where I fit best.

What type of roles am I targeting?
Data Analyst, BI Analyst, Power BI Developer, Finance Data Analyst and reporting-focused analytics roles.
What is my strongest technical area?
Power BI end-to-end delivery: Power Query, data modeling, DAX, dashboard design and business KPI implementation.
What business domains fit me best?
Finance, SaaS, operations, commercial analytics, project reporting and KPI-heavy management reporting.
Am I open to international roles?
Yes. I am based in Belgrade and open to remote or hybrid roles aligned with European working hours.
Do I offer freelance and consulting services as well?
Yes. I am open to freelance and consulting engagements where I can help with Power BI dashboards, reporting automation, KPI logic, Power Query transformations, dashboard redesign, and finance or operations reporting improvements. The best fit is practical, outcome-focused work where the goal is to turn scattered data into a clean reporting process or a dashboard people can actually use.
Am I available for full-time or part-time arrangements?
Yes. I can work with both full-time and part-time arrangements, depending on the client’s needs, preferred working model, and the scope of work. For larger BI ownership, reporting transformation, or long-term analytics roles, full-time makes sense. For focused dashboard development, reporting cleanup, automation, or consulting support, part-time or project-based collaboration can also work well.
